# Review and delivery

Review the selected forms; do not automatically produce or require every form.

## Source and learning content

- Read actual authorized source pages, figures and worked steps. Preserve originals and a source-to-concept coverage map.
- Check claims, conditions, units, reasoning and examples. Mark missing, unreadable or contradictory material.
- Distinguish source facts from derived explanations and original examples. Do not claim completeness or independent review without evidence.
- Review language meaning separately from physical alignment. The renderer positions supplied pairs; it does not certify their semantic equivalence.

## Unified lookup

- Find realistic remembered terms, aliases and abbreviations in one sorted headword sequence.
- Confirm that keywords matching a canonical headword are grouped with it, not moved to a separate mandatory index.
- Keep different senses explicit. Do not discard a substantive definition merely because a related target exists.
- Verify every route terminates at a concept or meaningful subentry, never another alias.
- Check the exact target title, context, local page and hyperlink. Subentry pointers must reach the subentry itself.
- When a companion is included, verify its document identity, section, printed page and named PDF destination. When omitted, no broken companion pointer may remain.
- Check first/last/continuation headers and fixed alphabet slots against the actual page contents, including route-only headwords.

## Decision paths

- Start from each intended entry question and exercise every ordinary choice and unknown route.
- Questions should be answerable from observable information; choices need clear distinctions.
- Clarifications state what to obtain before returning. Check their return conditions, rather than treating them as unconditional loops.
- Method leaves contain usable actions/checks. Required continuations are visible; terminal outcomes have none.
- Automated graph checks reject missing nodes, duplicate choices, empty routes and accidental forward cycles. They cannot establish that a method is correct or that all meaningful cases are covered.

## Layout and technical checks

Run the common renderer's acceptance checks, with geometry appropriate to the selected product. Body columns and navigation furniture have distinct intended regions; for tabs, verify declared rectangles and physical-edge inset as well as body geometry. Never lower a threshold merely to hide an unexplained failure.

Inspect every changed final page at readable resolution: aligned starts/rows, long-paragraph continuation and resynchronization, headings, references, RTL/CJK shaping, mixed-script identifiers, glyphs, clipping, images, folios and print parity. A first-page preview or successful compile is not a full review.

Keep automated unit/regression/integration tests alongside implementation changes. Preserve failure evidence and distinguish pass, failure, blocked, incomplete and unrun checks. Human factual, language and pixel review remains a separate qualified judgment.

## Delivery

Deliver the selected flat PDFs, portable native sources, required package/assets/licenses and source map. Keep relative cross-document targets together and report viewer limitations where relevant. State what was checked and what remains uncertain.

Public examples are original generic material. Before publication, inspect the actual source, images, PDF text/metadata and paths for private names, addresses, account data, source URLs, document identifiers and operational records. Do not copy private course content or project-specific format policy into the public skill.

The package does not authorize external processing or publication. Native TeX executes code; disabled shell escape does not isolate the filesystem. Observe the applicable source rights, permissions and dependency licenses.
